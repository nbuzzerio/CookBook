# Bundled visual assets

The three recipe-cover images in `public/assets/images/` were generated for Countertop with the built-in image-generation tool. They are illustrative food images, not photographs documenting a tested recipe. No remote image service is contacted by the app. Generated PNGs were encoded as quality-88 JPEGs for static delivery without changing their composition.

Shared prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: local premium editorial cookbook cover photograph. Scene: warm light limestone kitchen counter, subtle olive linen, natural rustic home cooking. Composition: landscape 3:2, closely framed overhead three-quarter angle, food centered and filling frame, inviting tactile realism. Lighting: soft natural side window light, warm ivory and sage palette, beautifully appetizing but believable. No people, no text, no watermarks, no logos, no graphic borders.

Subjects:

- `meatloaf.jpg`: Two separate rustic platters with sliced mushroom beef meatloaf and a smaller lentil mushroom loaf, deeply golden browned crust, moist natural interior, fresh thyme sprigs, sautéed oyster mushrooms, linen napkin. Beef and vegetarian dishes remain separate. No tomato glaze.
- `ziti.jpg`: An ivory ceramic casserole of gluten-free baked ziti with red tomato sauce, golden bubbling mozzarella, dark green spinach, and sautéed mushrooms. A serving spoon at the edge and loosely arranged basil leaves.
- `sausage.jpg`: A rustic ceramic platter of browned whole Italian sausages with vibrant strips of roasted red, yellow, and green bell peppers and translucent onions, herbs, and a little olive oil.

The original fallback illustration and favicon are code-native vector graphics created for this project. DM Sans and Libre Baskerville are bundled through their Fontsource packages; both use the SIL Open Font License. License copies are included in `public/licenses/`.
